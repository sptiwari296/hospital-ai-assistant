## 1. Medical emergencies
### 1.1 What to do
In a medical emergency inside the hospital, alert the nearest staff member or press the red emergency call button. For emergencies outside the hospital, call the national emergency number.

### 1.2 Emergency Department
The Emergency Department (Ground Floor, Block C) is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Patients are seen in order of medical urgency (triage), not arrival time.

## 2. Emergency codes
- **Code Blue:** cardiac or respiratory arrest – the resuscitation team responds.
- **Code Red:** fire – follow staff instructions and use the stairs, not lifts.
- **Code Pink:** missing infant or child – all exits are monitored.

## 3. Fire evacuation
Follow the green exit signs. Do not use lifts. Gather at the assembly point in the main car park.

## 4. Chatbot limitation
The patient assistant cannot handle emergencies. If you have chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding or loss of consciousness, go to the Emergency Department or call emergency services immediately.
