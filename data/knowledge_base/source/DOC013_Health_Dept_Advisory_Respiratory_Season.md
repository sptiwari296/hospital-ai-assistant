## 1. Background
Cases of seasonal respiratory infections are rising. Hospitals must reduce the risk of infection to vulnerable patients.

## 2. Directions to hospitals
### 2.1 Critical care visitors
In intensive care units (ICU) and high-dependency units, only **1 visitor** per patient is allowed at a time until this advisory expires.

### 2.2 Masks
All visitors must wear a medical mask inside clinical areas.

### 2.3 Visitors with symptoms
Visitors with fever, cough or sore throat must not visit patients.

## 3. Precedence
This advisory overrides any hospital policy that allows more visitors. Visiting hours set by hospitals are not changed by this advisory.
