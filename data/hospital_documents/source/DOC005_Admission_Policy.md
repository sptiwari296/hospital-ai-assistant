# Admission and Registration Policy

**Document ID:** DOC005 | **Version:** 2.1 | **Effective date:** 2026-03-01 | **Department:** Administration | **Approved by:** Medical Superintendent

*Synthetic document created for software development. Not real medical guidance.*

## 1. Purpose
To describe how patients are admitted to the hospital for planned (elective) and emergency care.

## 2. Planned admission
### 2.1 Before admission
- The treating doctor issues an admission advice note.
- The patient registers (see the Patient Registration SOP) and completes pre-admission tests if required.
- Insured patients submit the insurance card at the Insurance Desk at least 3 working days before admission for cashless approval.

### 2.2 On the day of admission
Report to the Admissions Desk (Ground Floor, Block A) at the time given in your admission letter. Bring your Patient ID, photo identity document, current medicines and previous reports.

### 2.3 Deposit
Patients without approved cashless insurance pay an admission deposit based on the estimated cost of the procedure and room type.

## 3. Emergency admission
Emergency patients are admitted directly through the Emergency Department. Registration and insurance formalities can be completed by a relative after the patient is stabilised.

## 4. Room allocation
Rooms are allocated by medical need and availability. Requests for a room upgrade are handled by the Admissions Desk.

## 5. Attendants
One attendant may stay with an admitted patient in general wards and private rooms. Attendants receive an attendant pass at admission.
