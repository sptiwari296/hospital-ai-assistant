# Insurance Agreement – Contoso Care Basic Plan

**Document ID:** DOC009 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2025-06-01 | **Department:** Insurance Desk | **Approved by:** Finance and Insurance Committee

*Synthetic document created for software development. Contoso Care is a fictional insurer.*

## 1. Parties
Agreement between the hospital and Contoso Care for members of the Basic Plan (annual limit INR 2,00,000).

## 2. General conditions
Coverage applies only while the policy is active. There is no waiting period for covered outpatient services.

## 3. Covered services
### 3.1 Consultations
General and specialist consultations are covered at 100 % up to INR 1,000 per visit.

### 3.2 MRI scans
MRI scans are **partially covered at 50 %** up to INR 10,000 per scan. No pre-authorisation needed.

### 3.3 Laboratory tests
Complete Blood Count is covered at 100 % up to INR 500.

## 4. Exclusions
### 4.1 Not covered
- Joint replacement surgery (including total knee replacement)
- Cosmetic procedures
