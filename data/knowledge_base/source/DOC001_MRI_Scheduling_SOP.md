# MRI Scanning – Scheduling and Preparation SOP

**Document ID:** DOC001 | **Version:** 1.2 | **Effective date:** 2026-10-01 | **Department:** Radiology | **Approved by:** Head of Radiology

*Synthetic document created for software development. Not real medical guidance.*

## 1. Purpose
This standard operating procedure explains how patients schedule an MRI scan and how they must prepare for it.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all outpatient MRI scans (brain, spine, knee and other body parts) performed in the Radiology Department.

## 3. How to schedule an MRI scan
### 3.1 Referral
An MRI scan can only be booked with a valid referral (medical order) from a hospital doctor. Self-referrals are not accepted.

### 3.2 Booking steps
1. Make sure your doctor has placed an MRI order in your medical record.
2. Book through the Radiology front desk (Ground Floor, Block B), the patient portal, or by calling the Radiology helpline.
3. Choose an available imaging slot. Slots are 30 minutes, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 13:00.
4. Complete the MRI safety screening questionnaire at least 24 hours before the scan.
5. If you have insurance, the Insurance Desk will check coverage before your scan date.

### 3.3 Rescheduling and cancellation
Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 24 hours before the scan without charge.

## 4. Safety screening
Patients must tell the Radiology team if they have a pacemaker, cochlear implant, metal fragments, an insulin pump, or are pregnant. Some implants make MRI unsafe; the radiologist decides whether the scan can proceed.

## 5. Preparation on the day
### 5.1 Fasting for contrast scans
For MRI scans with contrast, patients must not eat for **4 hours** before the scan. Water is allowed.

### 5.2 Arrival
Arrive 30 minutes before your slot. Remove all metal objects, jewellery and cards with magnetic strips.

### 5.3 Diabetic patients
Patients taking metformin who are scheduled for a contrast scan must inform the Radiology team; the referring physician will advise on medication.

## 6. Results
Reports are usually available within 48 hours and are shared with the referring doctor and on the patient portal.
