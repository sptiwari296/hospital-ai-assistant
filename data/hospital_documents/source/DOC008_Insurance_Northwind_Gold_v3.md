## 1. Parties
Agreement between the hospital and Northwind Health for members of the Gold Plan (annual limit INR 5,00,000). This version replaces version 2.0 from 1 January 2027.

## 2. General conditions
### 2.1 Active policy
Coverage applies only while the member's policy is active on the date of treatment.

### 2.2 Cashless treatment
Procedures that need pre-authorisation must be approved before treatment.

## 3. Covered services
### 3.2 Total knee replacement
- Covered at **90 %** up to **INR 4,00,000** per procedure.
- Pre-authorisation is required.
- Waiting period: 24 months from the date the member first joined the plan.

### 3.3 MRI scans
Covered at 100 % up to INR 25,000 per scan.

## 4. Exclusions
### 4.1 Cosmetic procedures
Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures are not covered.
