# Insurance Agreement – Northwind Health Gold Plan

**Document ID:** DOC007 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-04-01 | **Expiry date:** 2026-12-31 | **Department:** Insurance Desk | **Approved by:** Finance and Insurance Committee

*Synthetic document created for software development. Northwind Health is a fictional insurer.*

## 1. Parties
Agreement between the hospital and Northwind Health for members of the Gold Plan (annual limit INR 5,00,000).

## 2. General conditions
### 2.1 Active policy
Coverage applies only while the member's policy is active on the date of treatment.

### 2.2 Cashless treatment
Members can use cashless treatment through the hospital Insurance Desk. Procedures that need pre-authorisation must be approved before treatment.

## 3. Covered services
### 3.1 Coverage summary
Coverage is a percentage of the hospital charge, up to the maximum stated for each service.

### 3.2 Total knee replacement
- Covered at **80 %** up to **INR 3,50,000** per procedure.
- **Pre-authorisation is required.**
- **Waiting period: 24 months** from the date the member first joined the plan.
- A documented diagnosis of osteoarthritis of the knee by an orthopaedic surgeon is required.

### 3.3 MRI scans
Covered at 100 % up to INR 25,000 per scan. No pre-authorisation needed.

### 3.4 Diagnostic tests and X-rays
Laboratory tests, ECG and X-rays are covered at 80 % with no upper limit per test.

### 3.5 Consultations
Specialist and general consultations are covered at 100 % up to INR 2,000 per visit.

## 4. Exclusions
### 4.1 Cosmetic procedures
Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures (for example cosmetic rhinoplasty) are not covered.

## 5. Claims
The Insurance Desk submits claims directly to Northwind Health. Any amount not covered is payable by the patient.
