# Patient Registration SOP

**Document ID:** DOC002 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-15 | **Department:** Front Office | **Approved by:** Hospital Operations Manager

*Synthetic document created for software development. Not real medical guidance.*

## 1. Purpose
To register new patients accurately and create a unique Patient ID used across all hospital systems.

## 2. New patient registration
### 2.1 Documents required
- A government-issued photo identity document
- Contact phone number and address
- Insurance card (if the patient wants to use insurance)
- Previous medical records or referral letters (optional)

### 2.2 Steps
1. Visit the Registration Counter (Ground Floor, Main Lobby), open 08:00 to 20:00 every day.
2. Fill in the registration form or register online through the patient portal.
3. Staff verify identity and create a unique Patient ID (format P followed by four digits).
4. The patient receives a registration card and portal login details.

## 3. Returning patients
Returning patients only need their Patient ID or registered phone number. Staff must confirm two identifiers (name and date of birth) before sharing any information.

## 4. Updating details
Changes to address, phone number or insurance must be made at the Registration Counter with supporting documents.

## 5. Privacy
Registration staff must never read out identity numbers aloud and must only share patient information with the patient or an authorised representative.
