# ICU Visiting Policy

**Document ID:** DOC011 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01 | **Department:** Critical Care | **Approved by:** Medical Superintendent

## 1. Purpose
To protect critically ill patients while allowing family support.

## 2. Visiting hours
### 2.1 Times
ICU visiting hours are **11:00 to 12:00** and **17:00 to 18:00** every day.

### 2.2 Number of visitors
A maximum of **2 visitors** per patient at a time.

## 3. Rules
Visitors must wash their hands before entering and must not bring food or flowers.
