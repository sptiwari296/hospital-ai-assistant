# Nursing Care Guide – Preparing Patients for Procedures and Imaging

## 1. Purpose
To guide nursing staff in preparing patients for procedures, diagnostic imaging and surgery.

## 2. General preparation
### 2.1 Identity check
Confirm the patient's identity with two identifiers (full name and date of birth) before any procedure.

### 2.2 Allergies
Check and document allergies, especially to contrast agents, latex and antibiotics.

## 3. Imaging with contrast
### 3.1 Fasting
Patients scheduled for imaging with contrast (CT or MRI) must not eat for **6 hours** before the procedure. Small sips of water are allowed.

### 3.2 Kidney function
Check that a recent serum creatinine result is available for patients over 60 or with diabetes.

## 4. Vital signs
Record blood pressure, pulse, temperature and oxygen saturation before the patient is transferred.

## 5. Patient education
Explain each step in simple language and give written instructions. Confirm the patient understands by asking them to repeat the key points.
