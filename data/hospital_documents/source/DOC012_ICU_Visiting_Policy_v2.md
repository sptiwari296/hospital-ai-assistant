# ICU Visiting Policy

**Document ID:** DOC012 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-07-01 | **Department:** Critical Care | **Approved by:** Medical Superintendent

*Synthetic document created for software development. Not real medical guidance.*

## 1. Purpose
To protect critically ill patients while supporting family involvement in care. This version replaces version 1.0.

## 2. Scope
Applies to the Medical ICU, Surgical ICU and Cardiac ICU.

## 3. Visiting hours
### 3.1 Times
ICU visiting hours are **10:30 to 11:30** and **17:00 to 19:00** every day.

### 3.2 Number of visitors
A maximum of **2 visitors** per patient at a time. Visitors may swap with others in the waiting area.

### 3.3 Children
Children under 12 years are not allowed in the ICU.

## 4. Rules for visitors
- Wash or sanitise hands before and after the visit.
- Do not bring food, flowers or plants.
- Mobile phones must be on silent.
- Visits may be paused during medical procedures or emergencies.

## 5. Exceptions
The ICU consultant may allow visits outside these hours for end-of-life care or other special situations.
